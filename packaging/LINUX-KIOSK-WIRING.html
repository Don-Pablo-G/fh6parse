Change BCM numbers in kiosk settings (F2) or /etc/fh6parse-kiosk.ini. Overlay: ~/.config/fh6parse/ui.ini. Land these on the 52Pi screw terminal (same header numbers). Do not also push Dupont onto the 40-pin under the board.

7. Common ground

One GND net for encoders, every C tab, every LED −, and (if fitted) a Qwiic keypad. Tie them to any GND header pin listed above. Do not run a separate “button ground” to J2.

8. Identify vandal tabs with a meter

  1. Diode pair = LED + / − (one way ~1.8–3 V).
  2. Unpressed: C–NC beeps, C–NO open.
  3. Pressed: C–NO beeps. The tab that beeps in both tests is C.
Do not
  • Put 5 V on A / B / C / NO / NC or any BCM pin
  • Power EC11 VCC from pin 2 (5 V) unless a shifter is on A/B
  • Wire J2 to GPIO 25 or GPIO 20
  • Use RPi.GPIO on Pi 5 (RP1 needs lgpio)
  • Set WAIT_FOR_POWER_BUTTON=1 without a J2 (or reachable PCB) button
Without optional parts the kiosk still runs: idle blanks the panel; knobs / USB / keyboard wake it; USB-C plug-in boots the Pi. gpiozero pull-ups keep unused BCM 25 high.

9. Wires (52Pi screw terminal)

Pi GPIO end is the screw terminal board (Kamami 588019). Same 40-pin header as Pi 4B; pin 1 at USB-C. Brass M2.5 standoffs in the kit. If it will not clear the Active Cooler / FAN plug, sit it on a 40-pin GPIO riser (same pin 1). Silk IO17 = BCM 17. Leave header 3 / 5 empty (Qwiic). J2 is not on this board. GPIO sense, LED rings, and Pi power must never share a conductor that can put 5 V on a BCM pin. No jumper-wire packs.

Do not use the same red for RUN and for LED +. That mix-up is a dead Pi.

Terminations

  • Pi: screw terminal on the 40-pin header, or on a GPIO riser if the cooler is in the way. Brass standoffs. Strip ~5 mm, ferrule. Strain-relieve to the case, not the board.
  • Vandal: insulated 2.8 mm Faston females, crimped. Do not solder on the switch (the plastic melts). Leave NC empty.
  • EC11: Dupont or solder onto the pin row. Leave shaft C open.
  • J2: two short flying leads across the pads only. Never a screw on the GPIO block.

Length and noise

  • GPIO A/B and button NO under ~40 cm.
  • Twist each encoder pair. Do not tape encoder wires along the 5 V LED bundle or a USB 3 cable.
  • Star GND at header pins 6 / 9 / 14 — do not daisy LED − through a switch C then back to the Pi.
  • LED rings are tens of mA; pin 2 is enough. The official USB-C 5 V / 5 A cable is the only high-current wire.
  • Sleeve the run in PET braid. Hot-glue strain relief at the panel.

Must: no 5 V on BCM; PCIe flex seated, no crease; clamp USB-C + HDMI0 (no USB-C extension); PETG vents above cooler / below Duo; strain-relieve to the case. One kiosk: ferrule + Faston + braid. Terminal on a 40-pin riser if it will not fit. Official Pi 512 GB 2230 in Duo slot A. Panel USB 3.0 27 mm to Pi USB 3; P047 on USB 2.
